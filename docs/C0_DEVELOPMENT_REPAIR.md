## Implemented repairs

- Attribute the content of an explicit same-sentence “holder reported/denied/
  believed that subject …” clause to its named holder during preconstruction.
  The previous implementation could emit the embedded proposition as globally
  true merely because its predicate was not itself “reported.”
- Normalize office-state “served as” to “holds office” in C0's development
  dictionary. The scorer applies the same finite office-state lexical equivalence
  to every condition; appointment and succession events remain distinct.
- Match dictionary phrases on token boundaries: “revealed” must not match the
  shorter relation “led.”

No gold IDs, entity names, expected answers, thresholds, or query-time C0
construction operations were added. Unknown validity endpoints remain unknown.
No scorer temporal equivalence rule was changed.

## Reference-semantics problems requiring a separate decision

Among 67 same-evidence candidate comparisons, 66 differ in validity and 30
differ **only** in validity. The gold compiler intersects validity with the
query's story window, while C0/C1/FixedSelect preserve sealed qualifications.
The scorer currently requires exact interval equality. This is distinct from a
wrong extraction: clipping a supported open interval for scoring is not the
same as asserting that its underlying state ends at the window boundary.

Separately, some gold event/rare-fact validity endpoints come from latent
`WorldFactSpec.validity_end` values which `_render_fact` does not verbalize.
The development evidence supports story positions and temporal order but not
those exact endpoints. The detailed restricted examples distinguish these
unsupported intrinsic endpoints from legitimate query-window boundaries.

The current competence scorer also counts all predicted contextual assertions
against its filtered contextual reference set; presence of a fact evidence ID
alone does not establish that every required gold qualification is directly
stated. This needs resolution under the authoritative “directly stated
development qualified assertions” criterion, not a lowered threshold.

Proposed correction, **not applied**: establish an evidence-supported intrinsic
validity convention and a separately recorded query-window normalization for
scoring, applied symmetrically to every condition; regenerate/review affected
benchmark certificates only after approval. Preserve all original artifacts.
Do not infer missing durations or requalify C0/C1/FixedSelect after reveal.
